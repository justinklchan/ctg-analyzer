Which features actually carry the score?

The importance page ranks every feature by the size of its SHAP contribution, from one fit. This page refits the whole recipe many times over — each refit re-draws the fold split and re-randomises the booster — and asks which parts of that ranking survive, then checks the ranking against what the score actually loses when a feature is taken away.

“Important” is three different questions

Each column below answers one of them. A feature can be first on one and nowhere on another, and several here are.

Why they come apart. Mean |SHAP| is attribution: it divides the score's movement among the features that produced it. Where several features carry the same underlying signal, that division splits one signal many ways — each share looks modest, and each feature is individually removable because the others still carry it. Removal is the only one of the three that answers “does the score need this”, and it is also the one that is blind to a redundant family: dropping one deceleration feature leaves the other seven in place.

What this page cannot tell you

Drop-one cannot see a redundant family. Every deletion here removes exactly one column and leaves the other thirty-six. A family whose members substitute for each other will therefore show a whole row of harmless deletions while the family as a whole is load-bearing. The family-level ablation that would settle it has not been run.

The deletions are ranked, not exhaustive. Only the top few features by mean |SHAP| were refit without, because each one costs n refits. The rest are blank in that column — blank means not measured, not measured-and-harmless.

The univariate column ignores everything else. It is one feature against the outcome at the horizon shown, so a feature that only matters in combination scores low on it, and a feature standing in for the passage of labour scores high without adding anything the clock does not already say.

Stability is not correctness. A feature can sit at the same rank across every refit and every bootstrap because the model consistently leans on it for a reason that would not survive a different hospital. Everything here is one cohort — CTU-CHB, one hospital in Brno over 2010–2012 — re-drawn, never replaced.
